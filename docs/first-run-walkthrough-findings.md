# First-Run Walkthrough Findings

_Last updated: 2026-03-08_

This note captures the current first-run validation reading after the archetype/family hardening pass.

## Current click point

The most convincing moment is:

1. the user selects a visible trait in the preview
2. clicks `Mark Fixed` or `Allow Variation`
3. sees the role label, helper text, and `Example Family Member` update together

That is the point where the product stops reading like a generic graph surface and starts reading like a family-definition tool.

## Current strengths

- the launch copy now frames the product as archetype-first
- the quick starts express the same product model across character, vehicle, and building domains
- preview badges make fixed DNA versus allowed variation legible at a glance
- the family snapshot gives the graph a production-facing interpretation
- the example member now reads closer to a concise in-family description than a raw attribute pile

## Remaining risk to watch manually

- some prompts may still produce an example member that feels lightly assembled rather than fully natural
- users may still need one interaction before the family model really clicks
- if the parsed segments are awkward, the snapshot voice can only partially mask that

## Current judgment

The first-run surface appears aligned enough to stop expanding and move into manual verification.

If follow-up changes are needed, they should remain presentation-only:

- phrasing
- ordering
- emphasis
- natural-language assembly

They should not reopen:

- the family model
- broader PSG infrastructure
- scene/crowd/platform scope
