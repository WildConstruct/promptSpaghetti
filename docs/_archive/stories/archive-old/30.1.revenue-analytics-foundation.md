# Story 30.1 - Revenue Analytics Foundation

## Overview

Establish revenue tracking infrastructure by integrating Epic 1's analytics foundation with Epic 16's marketplace system to create comprehensive revenue analytics, payment integration analytics, and revenue dashboard capabilities.

## Business Value

- **Priority**: IMMEDIATE (Month 1)
- **Business Impact**: HIGH - Enable revenue tracking and optimization
- **Technical Risk**: LOW - Builds on established Epic 1 and Epic 16 foundations

## Dependencies

- ✅ Epic 1 (Analytics Foundation) - COMPLETE
- ✅ Epic 16 (Marketplace System) - COMPLETE

## Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue tracking schema integrated with Epic 16 marketplace data
- Real-time revenue dashboard with <2 second load times
- Payment analytics collection with automated reconciliation
- Revenue API endpoints with proper authentication and authorization
- Integration with Epic 1 analytics infrastructure maintained

## Technical Notes

- Leverage Epic 1's existing analytics pipelines for data processing
- Extend Epic 16's marketplace APIs rather than creating parallel systems
- Ensure revenue data consistency with marketplace transaction records
- Implement proper data retention and archival policies for compliance

## Estimated Effort

**Total: 10 developer days**

- Data model and integration: 3 days
- Dashboard implementation: 4 days
- Payment analytics: 3 days
