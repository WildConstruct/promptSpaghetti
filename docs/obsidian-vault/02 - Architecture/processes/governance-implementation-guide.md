## 7. Risk Management and Mitigation

### 7.1 Implementation Risks

```markdown
## Risk Assessment and Mitigation

### High Probability, High Impact Risks

#### Risk: Team Resistance to New Processes

- **Probability**: High
- **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation Strategies**:
  - Early and frequent communication
  - Involve team leads in process design
  - Start with pilot groups of willing participants
  - Demonstrate quick wins and benefits
  - Provide comprehensive training and support

#### Risk: Process Overhead Reduces Development Velocity

- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation Strategies**:
  - Streamline processes based on feedback
  - Automate repetitive tasks
  - Focus on value-add activities only
  - Measure and optimize review cycle times
  - Provide clear guidance on when processes apply

### Medium Probability, High Impact Risks

#### Risk: Inconsistent Application of Governance

- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation Strategies**:
  - Clear documentation and training
  - Automated enforcement where possible
  - Regular compliance audits
  - Leadership modeling of behavior
  - Recognition for good governance practices

#### Risk: Resource Constraints Limit Implementation

- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: Medium
- **Mitigation Strategies**:
  - Phased implementation approach
  - Focus on highest-impact activities first
  - Leverage automation to reduce manual effort
  - Cross-train team members to distribute load
  - Secure dedicated resources for implementation
```

### 7.2 Contingency Plans

```markdown
## Contingency Planning

### If Implementation Timeline Extends

- **Triggers**: Major delays, resource constraints, resistance
- **Response**:
  - Re-prioritize implementation phases
  - Focus on critical governance functions first
  - Extend timeline with stakeholder agreement
  - Increase automation to reduce manual overhead

### If Team Adoption is Low

- **Triggers**: <60% participation, negative feedback
- **Response**:
  - Conduct detailed feedback sessions
  - Simplify processes based on feedback
  - Increase training and support
  - Identify and address specific pain points
  - Consider process modifications

### If Quality Metrics Don't Improve

- **Triggers**: No improvement in key metrics after 6 months
- **Response**:
  - Conduct root cause analysis
  - Review process effectiveness
  - Adjust success criteria if needed
  - Implement additional quality measures
  - Consider external consultation
```
